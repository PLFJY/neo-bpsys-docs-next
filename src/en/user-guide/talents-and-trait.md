# Persona Talents and Traits

Use this page to set both sides' Persona talents and the Hunter's Trait for display on the transition overlay.

## Talent selector

This custom control consists of four ToggleButtons: click them to toggle each talent. Their positions match the in-game layout, so you can select them by muscle memory.

## Trait selection

Spectators in the game's tournament mode cannot see the Hunter's equipped Trait. The software therefore lets you choose whether to display it, for situations where it is known before play begins.

Click the Trait selected by the Hunter player.

![Hunter Trait selection](../../user-guide/images/TalentsAndTrait-HunTraitSelection.webp)
