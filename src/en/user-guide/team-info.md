# Team Information

Use this page to set team names, logos, starting players, and other team information.

## Set team information and starting players

![Set team information and starting players](../../user-guide/images/TeamInfo-TeamInfoPage.webp)

The screenshot shows the team information entry page. The away team uses the same procedure.

Fill in the team information to begin.

Click **Set as active** to make a player active. A maximum of four Survivor players and one Hunter player can be active at once. To bring another player in, first use **Set as inactive** on at least one active player of that faction.

You can also click **Import from JSON File** to [import team information in one operation](advanced/import-team-from-json.md).

## Change the player order {#修改选手顺序}

After setting both teams' starting players, use the buttons shown below to change the display order.

Click a number after a player's name to swap that player with the player in the specified position.

![Reorder starting players](../../user-guide/images/TeamInfo-ReorderActivePlayers.webp)
